## Features 新功能

1. Added changing the account email in Settings. Verify with the old email to change it right away, or with your password if the old email can no longer be used; the change then takes effect after 7 days. 设置中新增「更换邮箱」。可通过原邮箱验证立即更换；原邮箱无法使用时，可通过密码验证，7 天后更换生效。

## Fixes 修复

1. macOS: in-app updates now install when quitting with Cmd+Q. macOS 端使用 Cmd+Q 退出时，应用内更新现可正常安装。
2. Fixed requests failing with 401 on devices whose clock is off. 修复设备时间不准时部分请求失败（401）的问题。
3. Spaces at the start or end of a username are now removed before saving. 保存用户名时会去除首尾空格。
