## Enhancements 改进

1. Redesigned the Square: moments lie flat in one column with a thin line between them, on phones the Square and Announcements tabs head the page as its title, and on wide screens the announcements sit in a panel beside the moments. 重新设计广场：动态平铺为单列，以细线分隔；手机端广场与公告的标签作为页面标题置于顶部；宽屏下公告移至动态右侧。
2. On phones a bottom bar with three tabs (Home, Square, Me) replaces the top bar and the side drawer; the open tab wears the halo from the app icon. The logo and search open the home page, and the new Me page holds the library, messages, the creation, committee and contributor centers, and settings. 手机端以底部三个标签（主页、广场、我的）取代顶栏与侧边抽屉，当前标签头顶悬着图标里的那圈光环。Logo 与搜索位于主页顶部；新增的「我的」页收纳音乐库、消息、创作中心、委员会中心、贡献者中心与设置。
3. While pages load, placeholders in the shape of what's coming show where things will be, instead of a spinner, across the app: lists, details, comments, the creation, committee and contributor centers, and settings. When a page or a section fails to load, tap anywhere in it to try again; a failed search now says so instead of showing no results, and a profile that fails to load no longer leaves a blank page. 全应用的页面加载时以同形的占位代替转圈，涵盖各类列表、详情、评论、创作中心、委员会中心、贡献者中心与设置。页面或栏目加载失败时，点击该区域任意处即可重试；搜索失败时不再显示为「无结果」，个人主页加载失败时也不再是一片空白。
4. Searching from the home page now has a clear button that also leaves the search and closes the keyboard. 主页搜索新增清空按钮，点按后同时退出搜索并收起键盘。
5. Pull to refresh uses the app's own colors. 下拉刷新改用应用自身的配色。
6. In your own playlist, hold a song to drag it to a new place; the playlist can be deleted from the More menu at the top of its page. 在自己的歌单中，长按歌曲即可拖动调整顺序；可从歌单页顶部的「更多」菜单删除歌单。
7. On phones, pages below the top level open with their title large at the top, which folds into the bar as you scroll; the bar has no line under it until something scrolls beneath. Pages that play a list show the covers of their first songs fanned beside the title (at the end of the header row on wide screens), which play them all; the plain button takes their place in the bar once the title folds. Recent likes also say how many there are, over a row of the last 30 days with a bar for each day's likes; tap a day to go to it. On wide screens these list pages also head with a large title and a quiet line under it in place of a title in the bar, which shows it once you scroll. 手机端的二级页面以大标题开篇，向下滚动时标题收进顶栏；顶栏下的分隔线只在内容滚到其下方时出现。可整体播放的页面，标题右侧（宽屏为标题行末尾）斜叠着前几首歌的封面，点按即全部播放；标题收起后以播放按钮置于顶栏。「最近点赞」另列出总数，以及近 30 天每日点赞的细柱，点某一天即跳至当天。宽屏下这些列表页同样以大标题开篇，下附一行灰色说明，顶栏的标题在滚动后才出现。
8. Images in a moment open over the whole screen, system bars included. Pinch, double-tap or use the mouse wheel to zoom, drag a zoomed image to look around, and save it: to the photo album on phones, to a file of your choosing on desktop, as a download on the web. 动态中的图片全屏打开，覆盖系统栏；可双指、双击或滚轮缩放，放大后可拖动查看，也可保存：手机存入相册，桌面端另存为文件，网页端直接下载。
9. Brief notices appear as a plain pill in neutral black or white, opposite to the page, and go away when tapped. 简短提示改为中性黑白色调的圆角条，与页面明暗相反，点按即可收起。

## Fixes 修复

1. A song's comments open in one go: from the player they show the song at once, and opened by a link the page no longer swaps a first set of placeholders for a second. 歌曲评论一次载入：从播放器进入时歌曲信息立即显示；经链接打开时，页面不再先后出现两轮占位。
